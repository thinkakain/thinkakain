City Council Approves New Park Funding in 5-4 Vote

In a session lasting nearly four hours, the city council voted narrowly to allocate $2.3 million for the renovation of Riverside Park. The plan includes new walking trails, updated playground equipment, and expanded parking facilities.

"This is a win for families," said Council Member Alvarez. Opponents argued the funds should go toward road repairs instead. Construction begins in early spring.

Hiker Rescued After Shouting "Help!" for Three Hours

A 34-year-old hiker was rescued from the northern trails yesterday after becoming lost at dusk. "I just kept shouting 'Help!' until someone heard me," he told reporters. Search teams located him using his phone's GPS signal.

Officials remind hikers to carry emergency supplies and inform others of their planned routes.

Library Extends Weekend Hours

The downtown library will now open Sundays from noon to 5 PM, starting next month. The change comes after a petition gathered over 2,000 signatures.
