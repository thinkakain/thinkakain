Day 1 — Arrival at Petit Jean

Sunday, November 22, 2026

Drive: Allen → Petit Jean State Park, ~318 mi / ~5 hrs 40 mins via US-75 N and US-69 N through McAlester and Krebs, then across south-eastern Oklahoma and into Arkansas on AR-154.

Sleep: Cabin 004 — 1BR Duplex, 2 Queen beds. Reservation #1052856, $183.85 all-in. Check-in 4:00 PM. Guest name: Nuttanan Kunnitiphan. 📄 Reservation PDF — cabin 004

  • Lunch — Roseanna's Italian Food, Krebs, OK (205 E Washington Ave). Krebs is Oklahoma's "Little Italy", just east of McAlester and only a few minutes off US-69, about 108 mi / 1¾ hrs into the drive — leave Allen about 9:00 AM to arrive around 11:00–11:30 AM. Sunday hours are lunch only, 11:30 AM–2:30 PM. Family-run since 1975; the quarter-portion (pasta, ravioli, meatballs, a slice of lasagna and gnocchi) is the right size for a road-trip lunch, and the cheesecake is the famous finish. Back on the road by about 12:30 PM, Petit Jean around 4:00–4:30 PM. Alternative in the same town: Pete's Place (120 SW 8th St, Krebs), the landmark since 1925 — every party gets its own private dining room and every entrée comes with all-you-can-eat spaghetti, ravioli and meatballs first. Open Sunday 11:00 AM–8:00 PM, but allow at least 90 minutes and expect a much heavier meal, so only choose it if you want the full experience and a later arrival.
  • Easy first afternoon: Cedar Falls Overlook boardwalk
  • Red Bluff Drive and Petit Jean's grave overlooks at sunset
  • Dinner at the Mather Lodge restaurant
  • 🔥 Cabin 004 has a charcoal grill on the deck — charcoal is NOT provided. Bring charcoal, lighter and matches; buy them in Morrilton on the way up (no store at the park). Fireplace: a small bundle of wood is provided each day, extra bundles $3 from the lodge.

Day 2 — Petit Jean trails + cabin move

Monday, November 23, 2026

Sleep: Cabin 030 — Duplex, 2 Double beds. Reservation #1052857, $145.53 all-in. Check-in 4:00 PM. 📄 Reservation PDF — cabin 030

⚠️ Cabin move today: check out of cabin 004 at 11:00 AM, bags in the car, trails during the day, check in to cabin 030 at 4:00 PM. No single cabin covered both nights.

  • Morning — no rush: Breakfast at Mather Lodge and pack at leisure. 9:30–10:30 AM — Bear Cave + Rock House Cave: both easy ¼-mi trails, about ½ hr each, under a mile in total. Bear Cave is the giant sandstone boulders to scramble through and over; Rock House Cave is the big bluff shelter with Turtle Rocks and 500-year-old rock art. Back in plenty of time for check-out — drop one if running late. Optional add-on on the way back: Cedar Falls Overlook boardwalk, the falls from above before seeing them from below in the afternoon.
  • 11:00 AM: Check out of cabin 004, bags in the car, lunch at Mather Lodge.
  • Afternoon — main hike, 1:00–3:00 PM: Cedar Falls Trail — 2 mi round trip to the base of the 95-ft falls, about 2 hrs (steep climb back up). Start by 1:30 PM at the latest to finish in daylight.
  • 4:00 PM: Check in to cabin 030.
  • Sunset — 5:00 PM: CCC Overlook, on Red Bluff Drive. Be there by 4:40 PM. This CCC-built stone overlook clings to the edge of Cedar Creek Canyon and is the favourite sunset spot in the park — on a clear evening (forecast for the 23rd is mostly clear) you can see Mount Magazine and Mount Nebo in the distance as the sky turns. En route, the Mary Ann Richter Overlook on the same drive has big-sky views over the Arkansas River Valley. Closer alternative: Palisades Overlook on Highway 154, just across the canyon — an excellent sunset viewpoint over the Arkansas River Valley, and a shorter drive back if everyone is tired.
  • Dinner — after sunset, about 5:30 PM: Mather Lodge Restaurant. The only full dinner on the mountain, and the pick for tonight — no drive down to Morrilton and back up in the dark. Open until 8:00 PM in November (lunch and dinner 11:00 AM–8:00 PM). Southern menu: fried catfish, chicken-fried steak, burgers, ribeye, pulled pork, pecan pie. Takeout is available if the children would rather eat in the cabin. Note: Cabin 030 has no kitchen and no grilling is allowed (mini-fridge and microwave only), so cooking a full dinner in the cabin is not an option tonight — the grill plan only works in Cabin 004 on Sunday night. Other dinner options are down in Morrilton (Colton's Steak House, Yesterday's, Geo's Italian, Ortega's Mexican, all open until about 9:00 PM on a Monday), but they mean a dark, winding drive back up the mountain.
  • Note: The full Seven Hollows loop (4.5 mi) is dropped from today — it no longer fits comfortably with Cedar Falls in the afternoon. Museum of Automobiles only if time allows between hikes.

Sleep: Honeybee Geodesic Dome — Deluxe Dome, Starlight Haven (148 Jubilee Trail, Hot Springs). Hipcamp booking #4486638, $481.49 all-in. Check-in 4:00 PM, check-out Wed 11:00 AM. 4 guests.

  • Bathhouse Row — Fordyce Bathhouse (free National Park Service museum)
  • Grand Promenade — the quick trail for today: 0.5 mi, flat, paved, 15–20 min, easy. It runs right behind Bathhouse Row — a leg-stretcher after the ~1½-hr drive from Petit Jean, and it drops you straight at the Fordyce Bathhouse museum.
  • Alternative before leaving Petit Jean: Cedar Creek Trail — 1¼-mi loop, moderate, ~1½ hrs from Pioneer Cabin, following Cedar Creek above the falls. Only if you are out of the cabin by 9:30 AM, otherwise it rushes the drive.
